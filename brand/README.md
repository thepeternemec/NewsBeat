# Rarebird brand

**Market intelligence for stocks and crypto.**

Rarebird helps AI agents understand stock and crypto developments: what changed, why it could matter and what to check next.

[Website](https://www.rarebird.network) · [Project README](../README.md) · [Contact](mailto:contact@rarebird.network)

## Visual identity

The Rarebird identity uses the supplied outlined wordmark and the [R emblem](rarebird-emblem.svg). The emblem appears on a black tile at icon sizes.

- Use [the icon source](../apps/web/app/icon.svg) for the mark.
- Set the wordmark on its own in navigation, footers, and share cards.
- Use the R emblem on its own for app and browser icons, or as a separate element in an illustration.
- Use `BrandWordmark` and `BrandMark` from [brand-mark.tsx](../apps/web/components/site/brand-mark.tsx) for these placements.
- Use the site's theme variables for legible dark and light interfaces.
- Give the mark space and keep text readable at small sizes.

## Voice

Write clearly for someone trying Rarebird for the first time.

- Lead with what an agent can understand or check.
- Say **stocks and crypto** when describing coverage.
- Keep the reported change, interpretation and observed market evidence distinct.
- Describe the six-asset intelligence pilot as work in progress.
- Keep technical measurements in the methodology and optional detail.
- Preserve sources, timestamps, freshness and uncertainty.

Use **Rarebird** as one word. The brand domain is **rarebird.network**.
